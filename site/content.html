About the game

Across a wide, cel-shaded grassland lie five goblin caves. One day an elf with a harp arrives and the hunt begins. Elf & Goblins is a 1 vs 8 game of tag: play as the elf and capture every goblin within 10 minutes, or play as a goblin and help at least one of your crew survive until time runs out. Play solo against smart AI, or create a room and invite friends to play online — up to 9 players, no account and no download.

How to play

  1. Choose a side — the Elf or the Goblins. Elf players can pick the Harp Elf or the Amethyst Violinist.
  2. Elf wins by putting every goblin in the cage at the center of the map before 10 minutes are up.
  3. Goblins win if any goblin is still free when the timer ends.
  4. Move with WASD, drag the mouse to look, scroll to zoom. On phones and tablets, use the on-screen stick in landscape.

Characters and skills

Harp Elf — Sylvan Huntress

Faster than any goblin. Gaze fires a heart-eyed beam that roots goblins in place, Harp lures nearby goblins toward her and makes them quicker to capture, Stun knocks a goblin out at close range, and Capture bags a stunned goblin and sends it to the cage.

Amethyst Violinist

An assassin elf. Her violin turns her invisible for 15 seconds and frightens goblins when she reappears, Assassin's Eye reveals every goblin through walls and can teleport her behind the nearest one, and a 9-meter Charge stuns the first goblin in her path.

Cave Goblin

Small, clever and never alone. Goblins can Dash, Interfere to break a teammate out of the elf's sack, use a once-per-match Break Free when captured, and their Goblin Radar warns them when the elf walks into their cave.

FAQ

Is Elf & Goblins free?
Yes. It is free to play in any modern web browser with no download or sign-up.
Can I play with friends online?
Yes. Open Multiplayer, create a room and share the 4-digit code. Up to 9 players can join (1 elf and 8 goblins); empty goblin slots are filled by AI.
Can I play alone?
Yes. Close the multiplayer panel to play solo against AI goblins or an AI elf.
Does it work on mobile?
Yes. It runs on phones and tablets with touch controls; turn your device to landscape.
How long is a match?
Up to 10 minutes. The elf wins early if every goblin is captured.
What languages are supported?
English and Traditional Chinese.
